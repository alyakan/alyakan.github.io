so-cal privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026.

so-cal is a personal tool. I run it on my own computer to publish posts I have written and approved to my own social media accounts, including Threads. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

When I connect an account, so-cal receives an access token from that platform. For Threads it uses these permissions:

  • threads_basic, to read the connected account's username and confirm the token works.
  • threads_content_publish, to publish posts I have approved.

so-cal does not read other people's posts, messages, followers or any other data.

Where data is stored

Access tokens and post drafts are stored only on my computer, in local files. so-cal has no server, database or analytics, and it sends this data nowhere except to the platform it is publishing to.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Retention

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected or the token expires. Published posts stay on the platform under that platform's own policies.

Delete your data

so-cal's access can be removed, and its data deleted, at any time:

  • In Threads, open Settings, Account, Website permissions, and remove the app. This revokes its token immediately.
  • In so-cal, open Connections and choose Disconnect. This deletes the stored token from the computer.

For confirmation that data has been deleted, email aly.yakan@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy: aly.yakan@gmail.com. See also the terms of service.
