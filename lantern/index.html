Lantern for iPhone and Android

A remote control for Lantern on your Mac: see what your AI coding agents are doing, and answer them from anywhere in the room.

What it does

  • Shows your open chats and which of them are waiting on you.
  • Lets you approve or deny what an agent asks to do with a swipe, or read the whole command first.
  • Lets you answer an agent's questions, send it messages, wake a sleeping chat, and start a new chat in one of your projects.
  • Suggests what to do next when nothing is waiting.
  • Can notify you when a chat needs you or has finished.

It works with Lantern for Mac, which has to be running, on the same Wi-Fi as your phone. The phone talks to your Mac directly, encrypted, and to no server of mine. See the privacy policy.

Pairing your phone with your Mac

  1. In Lantern on your Mac, open Settings, then Remote control, and turn on "Let paired phones control Lantern".
  2. If macOS asks whether Lantern may accept incoming connections, choose Allow.
  3. Click "Show a pairing code".
  4. In the phone app, allow the camera and point it at the code. (Or use "Copy the link" on the Mac and "Paste the link instead" on the phone.)
  5. Confirm the Mac's name on the phone. A code works once, and only for a few minutes.

If the phone can't reach your Mac

  • Check that your Mac is awake, Lantern is running, and both are on the same Wi-Fi. A guest network or a VPN can keep them apart.
  • In Lantern's Settings, Remote control must be turned on.
  • Open System Settings, Network, Firewall on the Mac. If the firewall is on, Lantern has to be allowed to accept incoming connections. If you chose "Deny" once, change it there.
  • On iPhone, Settings, Lantern: "Local Network" must be on.
  • Tap "Try again" on the phone. If it still fails, unpair (tap your Mac's name on Home) and pair again.

Unpairing

In the phone app, tap your Mac's name on Home and choose "Unpair this phone". To also stop the Mac accepting that phone, remove it in Lantern's Settings, under Remote control.
